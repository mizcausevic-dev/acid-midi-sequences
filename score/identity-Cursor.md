# Study identity

Container lengths, tempos, and collections were fixed before note generation. No stochastic draws.

## s01 Offbeat Squelch

Sixteenth-note acid leaves every quarter-note downbeat empty and accents the offbeat. Variation B moves the attacks onto the downbeats and rests the offbeats.

Limitations: Use kick-a with variation A and kick-b with variation B. Glide is not encoded.

## s02 Tresillo Climb

A 3+3+2 sixteenth grid climbs the Phrygian mode. Variation B uses the opposite rest grid and descends.

Limitations: One quarter-note kick is shared. It does not mute the sequence.

## s03 Held Fifth

Variation A holds one note for most of each bar, alternating root and fifth. Variation B breaks those holds into two attacks a fifth apart.

Limitations: The long gates are note durations, not filter movement.

## s04 Short Gate

Variation A is a 90-tick engine that skips the downbeat of even bars. Variation B keeps only the odd sixteenths and a different four-note loop.

Limitations: Kick-a marks the skipped downbeats. Kick-b marks beat 3.

## s05 Question Gap

Two bars of eighth notes ask a question, then two bars leave a single offbeat answer. Variation B swaps which pair is dense.

Limitations: The kick follows variation A's gaps. Variation B does not keep those gaps.

## s06 Five Cell

A five-pitch cell rotates across a grid that skips two downbeats. Variation B retrogrades the cell on the opposite sixteenths.

Limitations: Kick-a fits variation A. Kick-b fits variation B.

## s07 Raised Seventh

Offbeat sixteenths lean on the raised seventh. Variation B moves those attacks one sixteenth earlier and descends.

Limitations: The raised seventh is a scale tone here, not a departure.

## s08 Dominant Leap

The line leaps to the raised third on a syncopated grid. Variation B delays the grid by three sixteenths and answers with a fall.

Limitations: Octave color is written as pitch, not as a synth octave switch.
